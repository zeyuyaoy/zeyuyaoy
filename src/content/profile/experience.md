## [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com/)

I founded my school’s Hack Club and helped grow Singapore’s network to 10+ clubs and 500+ young makers, supporting four new club launches. Through workshops and mentoring, I helped students build their first technical projects and supported club leaders across the Asia-Pacific region.

For High Seas, I developed project-evaluation tools and automated voting and scoring algorithms. In its first two months, the platform engaged 13,500+ students, who cast 65,000+ votes and shipped 5,000+ projects.

My work also spans outreach. For @horizons.arcana, I generated 101K+ Instagram views from 25.7K viewers in five weeks, with 1.1K+ shares.

## [BuildingBloCS](https://buildingblocs.sg)

I helped deliver national student-led computing conferences and hackathons reaching 3,700+ students across 75+ educational institutions. My contributions spanned outreach, website development, design, sponsorships, and on-site logistics.

I helped organize three AI-themed flagship hackathons for beginner through advanced programmers, alongside conferences at Singapore Management University featuring a fireside chat with Dr. He Ruimin, Singapore’s Chief AI Officer.

Across the 2025 programs, I helped expand our partner base, with support from organizations including MOE, NIE, SUTD, SMU, and GitHub.

## [IJHS & Young Achievers Leadership Academy](https://ijhscommunity.org)

At IJHS, I helped develop mentorship and leadership programs for a global community of 7,000+ scholars. I built onboarding and succession frameworks, mentored student leaders, and helped broaden gender and regional representation in recruitment.

I helped organize YALA programs bringing together 550+ participants from 40 countries, developed curricula and facilitator guides, and led the Project With a Purpose hackathon and workshops—helping students turn ideas for underserved communities into practical projects.

I also built the IJHS community website and automation tools for a 300+ member international Discord community.
